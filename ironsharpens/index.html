Support

Thanks for using Iron Sharpens to help others talk openly about their faith. Here's how to reach us if anything isn't working as expected.

Contact us

The fastest way to get help is by email. We read every message and aim to respond within 1–3 business days.

rollinseymour@icloud.com

When reporting a bug

To help us reproduce and fix the issue quickly, please include:

  • Your device model (e.g., iPhone 15, iPad Air)
  • iOS version
  • App version (found in the App Store)
  • A short description of what you expected vs. what happened
  • Screenshots, if possible

Frequently asked questions

What is Iron Sharpens?

Iron Sharpens is a card-game collection with over 500 prompts designed to spark meaningful, faith-centered conversations. Inspired by Proverbs 27:17 — “As iron sharpeneth iron, so a man sharpeneth the countenance of his friend.”– it's great for small groups, families, friends, and one-on-one discipleship.

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. Iron Sharpens is designed to work offline — all of the prompts are included with the app, so you can play anywhere.

Does the app show ads?

No. Iron Sharpens is ad-free.

Is it suitable for all ages?

Iron Sharpens is written for teens and adults engaging in thoughtful faith discussions. Many prompts work well for family and youth-group settings; we recommend leaders preview prompts to choose what best fits their group.

Do you collect any personal information?

No. The app does not require an account, shows no ads, and your activity stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.

How do I request a feature or suggest a new prompt?

We'd love to hear your ideas. Email us with the subject “Feature Request” or “Prompt Idea” and we'll consider it for a future update.
