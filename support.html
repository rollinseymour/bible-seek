Support

We're glad you're using our apps. Here's how to reach us if anything isn't working as expected.

Contact us

The fastest way to get help is by email. We read every message and aim to respond within 1–3 business days.

Send your question, feedback, or bug report to:

rollinseymour@outlook.com

When reporting a bug

To help us reproduce and fix the issue quickly, please include:

  • Which app you're using (Bible Seek or Bible Games)
  • Your device model (e.g., iPhone 15, iPad Air)
  • iOS version
  • App version (found at the bottom of the app's home screen or in the App Store)
  • A short description of what you expected vs. what happened
  • Screenshots, if possible

Frequently asked questions

Do the apps require an internet connection?

No. Both Bible Seek and Bible Games are designed offline-first. The full Bible text and all game content are bundled with the app. Bible Games shows banner ads when an internet connection is available, but the games themselves work entirely offline.

Are the apps suitable for children?

Yes. Both apps are designed for all ages. We use family-friendly ad content settings (G-rated, child-directed) for any advertising shown.

Do you collect any personal information?

We collect very little. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.

How do I request a feature or content addition?

We love hearing ideas. Email us at the address above with the subject "Feature Request" and we'll consider it for a future update.

Will my purchase / progress carry across devices?

Currently, game progress is stored locally on your device. We may add iCloud sync in a future update.
