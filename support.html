Support

We're glad you're using Bible Games: Suite. Here's how to reach us if anything isn't working as expected.

Contact us

The fastest way to get help is by email. We read every message and aim to respond within 1–3 business days.

Send your question, feedback, or bug report to:

bibleseek@icloud.com

When reporting a bug

To help us reproduce and fix the issue quickly, please include:

  • Your device model (e.g., iPhone 15, iPad Air)
  • iOS version
  • App version (found at the bottom of the home screen or in the App Store)
  • Which game you were playing when the issue occurred
  • A short description of what you expected vs. what happened
  • Screenshots, if possible

Frequently asked questions

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. Bible Games: Suite is designed offline-first. All six games and their content are bundled with the app. Banner advertising is shown when an internet connection is available, but the games themselves work entirely offline.

Is the app suitable for children?

Yes. The app is designed for all ages. We use family-friendly ad content settings (G-rated, child-directed) for any advertising shown.

Do you collect any personal information?

We collect very little. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.

How do I request a feature or content addition?

We love hearing ideas. Email us at the address above with the subject "Feature Request" and we'll consider it for a future update.

Will my game progress carry across devices?

Currently, game progress is stored locally on your device. We may add iCloud sync in a future update.

Which Bible translations are included?

Bible Games: Suite uses verses and references from the American Standard Version (ASV) and the King James Version (KJV), both of which are in the public domain.
