Bible Games: Suite

Bible-themed games built for all ages, fully offline. Read, learn, and have fun with Scripture together.

Seven games:

Test your knowledge

Bible Trivia

Hundreds of questions across three difficulty levels covering both Old and New Testament.

List the Books

Books of the Bible

Reorder the books of the Bible against the clock. Old Testament, New Testament- or both.

Memorize Scripture

Verse Scramble

Reorder shuffled words from popular Bible verses. Helps with memorization and recall.

Identify the figure

Who Am I?

Read clues from the life of a Bible character and guess who they are. 50+ characters across difficulties.

Find hidden words

Word Search

Hunt down Bible-themed words in a grid of letters. Multiple themed word lists.

Guess the word

Hangman

Classic Hangman with curated Bible-themed word lists, organized by difficulty.

Match the pairs

Memory Match

Flip cards to find matching Bible-themed pairs. 50+ pairs per difficulty with quick preview before play.

Features

  • Designed for all ages — from kids to grandparents
  • Fully offline — no internet connection required to play
  • No accounts, no in-app purchases, no nonsense
  • Family-friendly ad content (G-rated, child-directed settings)
  • Hundreds of questions, characters, words, and verses
  • Includes both ASV and KJV Bible references
