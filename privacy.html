Privacy Policy

Last updated May 8, 2026

The short version. Bible Games: Suite is designed to respect your privacy. We do not require accounts, do not ask for your name or email, and do not track your gameplay. The app itself does not collect personal data. Banner advertising in the app is provided by Google AdMob and is configured to family-friendly content only.

1. Information we collect

Bible Games: Suite is offline-first and account-free. The app does not ask you for, transmit, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

Game progress (such as your high scores and difficulty preferences) is stored locally on your device using standard iOS preferences. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to back up your device through iCloud or iTunes, in which case the backup is governed by Apple's privacy practices.

2. Advertising

Bible Games: Suite displays banner advertising provided by Google AdMob to support continued development of the app. To improve the relevance and appropriateness of ads shown, we have configured AdMob with the following settings:

  • Maximum content rating: G (General audiences) only
  • Child-directed treatment: Enabled (COPPA-compliant)
  • Under-age-of-consent flag: Enabled (additional safeguards for minors)
  • Personalized advertising: Disabled

Google AdMob may collect limited technical information to serve ads, such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type, and operating system. Google's use of this information is governed by their own privacy policy:

How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

3. Children's privacy

Bible Games: Suite is rated for all ages and is suitable for children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. As described above, advertising is configured to family-friendly settings and child-directed treatment is enabled.

If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns about your child's use of the app, please contact us using the email below.

4. Information we do not collect

To be explicit, Bible Games: Suite does not collect any of the following:

  • Account credentials, passwords, or login information (we don't have accounts)
  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Location information
  • Photos, contacts, or device storage contents
  • Health, financial, or biometric data
  • Browsing history outside of the app

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal data with third parties because we do not collect any. The only third party with access to limited technical information is Google AdMob, as described in the Advertising section above.

6. Data security

Because the app does not transmit personal data to our servers, there is no server-side data of yours for us to safeguard. Local game progress on your device is protected by your device's standard iOS security features.

7. Your choices

  • Limit Ad Tracking: You can reset or disable your device's advertising identifier by going to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iOS device.
  • Uninstalling the app: Removes all locally stored game progress.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be communicated through the app or this page.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your privacy in the app? Email us at bibleseek@icloud.com.
